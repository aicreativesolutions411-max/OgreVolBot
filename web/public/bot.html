GOOD TO KNOW

Before you jump in.

Is Buy Bot the same as buying a coin?

No. Buy Bot publishes alerts for a tracked coin. To place your own trade, open the bot privately or use a scan’s Quick Buy flow with your own wallet and settings.

Can I manage several groups without flooding their chats?

Yes. Open /groupsettings in DM and choose a room you administer. Add a coin address, title, @username or chat ID to find it faster. The bot must already be added and your admin access is checked.

Does it support Solana and Robinhood Chain?

Scanning supports both. Solana has direct Telegram Quick Buy flows; Robinhood scans provide a Trade Pad handoff for supported routes. Orders, wallets and launch options depend on the chain and market. Verify the full address—tickers are not unique.

Does opening the bot spend money?

Opening this page or its bot links does not submit a trade. Buying, selling, launching or enabling automation can spend funds and incur platform, network or routing fees. Review the current amount, fees and presets before enabling a paid action.

Why might a scan have missing data?

New coins and markets take time to be indexed. Prices, holder data, history and alerts depend on providers. Missing data is not a clean bill of health, and no scanner can guarantee safety or a successful sale.

How do I keep my wallet and group safe?

Check the exact handle: @SlimeWiredBot. Keep backups private. Never send a seed phrase, private key or login code to a group or support account. Grant only needed admin permissions. Automated trades can lose money.
