What am I claiming?

SOL from SlimeWire-managed creator-fee allocations. This is not X Money, cash, a bank transfer or an endorsement by X or the recipient. Fees depend on actual trading activity; income is not guaranteed.

Allocations normally run on the coin’s 12-hour rewards cycle and may be delayed by missing data or network issues. At least 0.001 SOL is needed per coin to claim; smaller amounts remain reserved. The creator funds network fees. You cannot claim by typing somebody else’s handle.

Entitlements follow the verified numeric X account ID, including after a username change. Keep your X account secure; SlimeWire does not reassign fees using screenshots or display names. Account-access recovery must happen through X. The dedicated rewards vault is managed by SlimeWire; it is not a self-custodial claim contract.
