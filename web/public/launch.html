What can I launch with today? +

Launch a SOL-paired Pump coin with artwork, socials, bundles, participant invites and an optional NFT collection. Keep your creator fees, reward your own community, or reward yours and another coin’s community. Holder snapshots run every 12 hours, with proportional rewards for wallets holding strictly more than $20. Missing data can delay payouts; small balances accumulate. You can also paste a Solana receiving wallet and select its percentage in the same split. Its payouts need no signup or holder balance; all selected percentages total 100%.

All fees and rewards here are in SOL. Another recipient community does not change the trading pair. Other crypto pairs, restricted stock-token pairs, NFT floor buying and new Cashback coins are not enabled. Existing claims and recovery records remain intact. Naming a community is not proof of partnership.
