BEFORE YOU DIVE IN

Know what you hold.

What do holders receive?

The selected crypto pairing is also the reward asset: STONK rewards on a STONK pair, JUP rewards on a JUP pair, and so on. Reward launches use a 1% or 3% transfer fee where offered. Amounts and timing depend on actual transfers, conversion liquidity and external distribution rules. Nothing here guarantees a return or a fixed payment schedule.

How do creator fees and holder rewards differ?

Choose one fee model. Standard launches have creator fees and no transfer tax. Holder-reward launches fund a holder pool instead and have no separate creator-fee position; trading fees go to the platform. Pending rewards are not wallet funds. SOL is still needed for transaction fees, even when the pairing and rewards are another token.

Can two communities share rewards?

On supported pairings, community mode allocates 67% of the holder-reward pool to the launched coin’s holders and 33% to the quote-token community. Both receive the selected quote asset. The current configuration is checked in the planner; USDC and wrapped SOL currently offer own-holder rewards only. This does not inherit the regular launchpad’s SOL-only splits, $20 threshold or twice-daily schedule.

Is a paired coin the same as a stock?

No. This planner offers reviewed crypto mints, not stock-backed assets or direct ownership of company shares. A shared ticker or pairing does not imply backing or affiliation. Historical creator lookups may contain other assets and are read-only.

Availability, data and risk

The current external transaction infrastructure excludes citizens or residents of, or people located in, the United States, Canada or United Kingdom, sanctioned jurisdictions, or wherever prohibited. Using a crypto pairing does not remove those service restrictions. Business entities are also subject to eligibility requirements.

SlimeStonks is a SlimeWire interface using external market and reward infrastructure. Entire-network figures include assets beyond this crypto list and are not SlimeWire earnings. Data can lag, indexed assets are not endorsements, and reward mechanisms can change, pause or end. This preview cannot launch, swap or claim. SlimeWire’s existing wallet and launchpad remain separate.
