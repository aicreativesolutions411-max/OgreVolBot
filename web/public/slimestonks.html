BEFORE YOU DIVE IN

Know what you hold.

How would SlimeWire token rewards work?

In the new setup preview, choose an eligible Solana token by ticker or exact mint. That same token is the pair currency and payout currency. Set developer-only, holder-only, or a percentage split with another community. The independent launch configuration uses a 1% trading fee and no transfer tax. It is not active yet.

The planned distributor sends realized net fees every 12 hours, with proportional holder allocations for wallets holding more than $20 at the snapshot. Missing prices, incomplete snapshots or an unfunded SOL expense budget delay payouts; unpaid amounts remain reserved. A plan is not a promise of earnings.

Why do existing market coins show different fee rules?

The market browser displays externally indexed coins, not launches made by the new SlimeWire reward setup. Their existing rules stay unchanged: standard coins have creator fees without transfer tax, while supported holder-reward coins use a 1% or 3% transfer fee and no separate creator-fee position. External conversion and distribution rules determine amounts and timing.

Supported external two-community coins may use a 67% / 33% holder-pool split. They do not inherit SlimeWire’s customizable split, $20 eligibility threshold or proposed twice-daily schedule. Always inspect the individual coin’s configuration and confirmed records.

Can I choose any Solana token?

You can search beyond the preset list, but a match is not automatic approval. The exact address must be an initialized original SPL mint with supported precision, no freeze authority, sufficient indexed liquidity and a valid launch curve. Token-2022 payout assets are not enabled. Unsupported tokens show a reason; we never silently replace your selection with SOL.

Mint checks do not establish value, issuer approval or investment safety. SOL is still needed for network fees and token-account rent. A SOL pair pays wrapped SOL to token accounts.

Is a paired coin the same as a stock?

No. The new setup offers crypto-token pairings, not stock-backed assets or direct ownership of company shares. A shared ticker or pairing does not imply backing or affiliation. Token issuer eligibility and applicable restrictions still apply.

Availability, data and risk

The external transaction infrastructure excludes citizens or residents of, or people located in, the United States, Canada or United Kingdom, sanctioned jurisdictions, or wherever prohibited. This remains unchanged. The separate SlimeWire reward route is not enabled and does not grant approval to use restricted assets or services.

Entire-network figures include assets beyond this crypto list and are not SlimeWire earnings. Data can lag; indexed assets are not endorsements. Both planners are currently read-only: no launch, swap, claim or automatic payout is activated here. The existing wallet and regular launchpad remain separate.
