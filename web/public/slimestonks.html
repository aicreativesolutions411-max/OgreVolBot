BEFORE YOU DIVE IN

Know what you hold.

Is a paired coin the same as a stock?

No. A community coin trading against a tokenized asset is not that asset, and is not direct ownership of company shares. Tokenized quotes have their own issuer, redemption terms and restrictions. A similar name or ticker is not proof of backing or affiliation.

How do creator fees and holder rewards differ?

Standard launches and reward launches have different fee models. Some creator payments are forwarded automatically; other older positions require a claim. Reward coins can charge a transfer fee. The coin view keeps paid, pending, base-token and quote-token amounts separate. Pending amounts are not wallet funds.

Can two communities share rewards?

Community mode is available only on supported quote assets. Its current percentage is shown in the launch planner, not assumed from another launch product. It is a share of holder payouts, not direct stock dividends.

Availability, data and risk

Transactional services are not available to citizens or residents of, or people located in, the United States, Canada or United Kingdom, sanctioned jurisdictions, or wherever prohibited. Business entities are also subject to eligibility requirements. Token and issuer rules may impose additional restrictions.

SlimeStonks is a SlimeWire interface using external market and reward infrastructure. Network-wide figures are not SlimeWire earnings. Data can lag, indexed assets are not endorsements, and reward mechanisms can change, pause or end. This preview cannot launch, swap or claim. SlimeWire’s existing wallet and launchpad remain separate.
