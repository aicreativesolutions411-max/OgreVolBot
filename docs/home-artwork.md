# SlimeWire homepage artwork

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-09-28, using the
user-selected black/lime homepage reference. Text, navigation, buttons and
accessibility semantics are real HTML, not part of the generated images.

Shipped assets (WebP, quality 86, same dimensions as generated PNGs):

- `web/public/assets/slimewire/home/wallet-v1.webp` — 1774 × 887
- `web/public/assets/slimewire/home/launch-v1.webp` — 1774 × 887
- `web/public/assets/slimewire/home/terminal-v1.webp` — 1774 × 887

Shared generation prompt:

> Use case: product-mockup. Create a production website background asset derived
> closely from the supplied SlimeWire homepage design reference. Output just
> artwork for ONE card, not an interface/screenshot. Landscape 2:1, premium
> black/deep forest/acid-lime glass reflections. Left 42% almost black and empty
> for HTML copy. No buttons, browser, headings, UI, letters, words, watermark or
> borders. Match elegant photorealistic 3D materials and restrained black/green
> lighting.

Card-specific direction:

- Wallet: realistic diagonal black leather wallet in the right 58%, chrome
  edges and black/green liquid, entire silhouette visible, no lettering.
- Launch: translucent lime glass rocket upright on a dark cylindrical pedestal
  at right, glossy liquid, sparse droplets, full rocket visible, no lettering.
- Terminal: decorative finely spaced green financial candles across the right
  two-thirds, subtle grid and flowing black/green wave; no data, labels or monitor.

The Claim fees card reuses `assets/slimewire/launch/liquid-obsidian-v1.webp`.
Decorative candles are artwork, not live market data. No extra X identity or
payment capability is implied by the homepage: its claim chooser links to the
existing wallet creator-fee view and community rewards inbox.
